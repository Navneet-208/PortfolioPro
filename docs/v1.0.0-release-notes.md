# PortfolioPro - Version 1.0.0 Release Notes
**Deliverable D17 (Sprint 3 - Epic P15-E12 / US37)**

**Release Version:** `v1.0.0`  
**Release Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Build Status:** Production Ready  

---

### Highlights & Summary
We are proud to announce the initial official release of **PortfolioPro v1.0.0**, an advanced stock trading and portfolio management platform. This milestone fulfills all deliverables and user stories specified in the ABES MCA project specification (`guide2.pdf`).

### Included Features by Module

#### 1. User Authentication & Account Management (Epics P15-E02 & P15-E03)
- Full user registration and secure login supporting dual roles: **Customer / Trader** and **Finance Officer / Auditor**.
- Automatic provisioning of a paper trading account credited with **$100,000 USD** virtual capital.
- Simulated cash deposit and withdrawal system with real-time balance reconciliation.

#### 2. Market Data Feed & Equities Screener (Epic P15-E04)
- Live institutional quote display across 12 equities covering Technology, Financials, Healthcare, Consumer Cyclicals, Energy, and Communications.
- 30-day historical OHLCV candlestick series for technical charting.
- Search filter and sector category chips.

#### 3. Paper Trading Execution Engine (Epic P15-E05)
- Support for **Market**, **Limit**, and **Stop-Loss** orders.
- Immediate paper fill execution with automatic weighted average cost calculation.
- Real-time balance and position validation preventing overdrafts or negative share selling.
- Full immutable trade execution audit trail.

#### 4. Real-Time Portfolio Accounting (Epic P15-E06)
- Consolidated account equity, cash reserves, and market exposure.
- Unrealized P&L dollar amounts and return percentages calculated per position.
- Asset allocation doughnut visualization powered by Chart.js.

#### 5. Quantitative Risk Analytics (Epic P15-E07)
- 1-Day 95% Parametric Value at Risk (VaR) computation.
- Weighted portfolio beta ($\beta$) calculation against the benchmark S&P 500.
- Sharpe ratio and annualized volatility metrics.
- 3 macroeconomic stress test simulations (2008 Financial Crisis, Tech Sector Correction, Fed Rate Hike).

#### 6. Algorithmic Technical & Fundamental Analysis (Epics P15-E08 & P15-E09)
- Real-time computation of 20-Day SMA, 50-Day SMA, 20-Day EMA, 14-Day RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands (±2σ).
- Algorithmic trade signals: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, Strong Sell.
- Corporate balance sheet health ratios: ROE, Net Margin %, Debt-to-Equity, and Quick Ratio.

#### 7. Notifications & Customer Support Desk (Epics P15-E10 & P15-E11)
- Automated alerts for order executions and price threshold crossings.
- Complete support ticketing workflow with priority tags and threaded messages between clients and staff.

#### 8. Documentation & Testing (Epic P15-E12)
- 13-page academic and industrial project documentation report.
- 13 passed automated pytest suites.
- OpenAPI 3.0 / Swagger UI documentation at `/api/docs/`.
